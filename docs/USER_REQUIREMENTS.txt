## 11. Cash-reporting workflow

When James is absent:
1. Jake pays Bob.
2. Bob writes a numbered paper acknowledgment and retains a copy.
3. Jake selects “Report a payment.”
4. The screen states: “This website does not accept payments. Report money or a check already handed to an authorized collector.”
5. Jake selects Cash or Check.
6. He enters amount, actual handover date, recipient, optional paper reference, intended rental months, and an optional note.
7. The report becomes Pending verification.
8. James matches the report with Bob’s retained record or confirmation and collection handover.
9. Authorized staff verifies the appropriate receipt/settlement state.
10. The system produces the appropriate acknowledgment.

Cash existing in the cash box does not establish who paid.

Report statuses:
- Pending verification.
- Needs clarification, with explanation.
- Verified.
- Cancelled.

Reports are claims, not settled payments. A pending report does not reduce official balances.

Show:
“Recorded balance: ₱8,000.”
“Reported cash awaiting verification: ₱4,000.”
“Your recorded balance updates after verification.”

Preserve actual payment date, submission time, verifier, and verification time separately.

James may enter payments directly without a renter report.

Allow reports to be matched to already-recorded payments so late submissions do not duplicate payments.

Use appropriate duplicate warnings without incorrectly preventing legitimate same-amount payments.

## 12. Checks and settlement

Separate:
- Tenant report.
- Instrument receipt.
- Settlement of funds.

For cash, verified receipt can settle the payment.

For checks:
1. Authorized collector receives the check.
2. Staff verifies physical receipt.
3. Staff records deposit where applicable.
4. Authorized staff records bank-confirmed clearance.
5. Only cleared funds reduce settled outstanding balances.

Check states:
- Received.
- Deposited / awaiting clearance.
- Cleared.
- Returned / dishonored.
- Cancelled.

Report verification and check clearance are distinct states.

Show “Check received — awaiting clearance,” not “Paid.”

Capture only needed details:
- Amount.
- Receipt date.
- Check date.
- Bank name.
- Check number.
- Payer.
- Collector.
- Deposit date.
- Clearance date.
- Staff identity for transitions.

Do not collect bank account numbers or check photos initially.

Do not automatically clear checks based on elapsed time.

Preserve intended allocations while pending. Revalidate balances at settlement and handle excess under the agreed credit policy.

Clarify postdated checks, timely-payment rules, returned-check fees, and authority to clear/reverse checks.

A returned check does not count as settled funds. A reversal after recorded clearance must be explicit and auditable.
